### 🗨️ About
Geckos is a mod that adds adorable and detailed geckos to your world, bringing a small but immersive companion experience.  
  They can be found in common biomes such as **Forest, Plains, Savanna, and Desert**, each with its own unique variation.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/eed10413a8deafcf1cd0f973c2b35bc61499dbba.png" width="450">
</p>

### 🦎 Taming
You can tame geckos using a dead fly obtained by killing a fly.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/motomoto-bugggg/assets/blob/main/output-onlinegiftools-com(2).gif?raw=true" width="450">
</p>

### 🌙 Behavior
Geckos sleep during the night and regenerate health while resting.  
You can also make your gecko sleep by clicking on it.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c7531783546bb5134ab20a204c6463fb5d305afc.png" width="450">
</p>

### 🐾 Companion Feature
You can pick up your Gecko onto your shoulder or head by looking at it and pressing the assigned key. Geckos may also climb onto you on their own.


*For a better first-person experience, it is recommended to use the First-Person Model mod.*

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c80c8574fb2b02117d04038e3e24858f9a4fba2f.png" width="450">

### 📜 Gecko HUD

When looking at your Gecko, a panel will appear, allowing you to move your Gecko between your shoulder and head.
